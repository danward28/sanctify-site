iPhone and iPad

Install the filter, then turn on the Safari experience.

01

Download the Apple setup file.

Open Protect in Sanctify, choose iPhone, and tap Download iPhone setup file.

02

Approve it in Settings.

Open Profile Downloaded in Settings, tap Install, enter the device passcode, and finish the prompts.

03

Enable Sanctify in Safari.

Go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Sanctify. Turn it on and allow it on all websites.

04

Connect the family policy.

Create a one-time Safari code in Protect, open the Sanctify extension in Safari, and enter the code within ten minutes.

Finish with proof

Know that protection is actually on.

How do I test without opening an unsafe website?

Use the safe test from the signed-in guide. On a supported browser or Android device, it opens the calm Sanctify page. It never loads harmful content.

Why did I see a normal connection error?

The filtering layer may be active while the local Sanctify experience is not. On Android, check intervention and alert permissions. In Safari, Chrome, or Edge, confirm the Sanctify extension is enabled and paired.

Will the calm page appear on every screen?

Android can surface it after supported blocked requests. Safari, Chrome, and Edge display it through their local protection. Televisions, consoles, and restricted app screens may show their normal connection message while the unsafe destination remains blocked.

Do I need an invitation code or a congregation?

No. Every family can use Sanctify and subscribe without either one. Linking a congregation is optional and can be completed later.

What should happen when I choose a plan?

Selecting Subscribe Now in account Settings opens Stripe's secure checkout. If it does not open, stay signed in and contact support. Do not create another account to work around the problem.

How do I remove Sanctify?

The signed-in setup page includes removal steps for the Apple profile, Android protected connection, browser extension, computer, and router.
